Privacy Policy

AI YouTube Factory · Effective 7 October 2026

AI YouTube Factory ("the app") is a private tool operated by its developer for the developer's own YouTube channel. The only Google account that authorizes the app is the developer's own.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

  • Channel identity: channel ID and title, used to confirm uploads go to the correct channel.
  • Videos uploaded by the app: video IDs, titles, descriptions, privacy and processing status.
  • YouTube Analytics for the channel's own videos: aggregated metrics such as views, watch time, likes, shares and subscribers gained. No information about individual viewers is requested or stored.

How data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to upload the developer's videos and to measure their performance.
  • Video records and aggregated metrics are stored in the developer's private database. OAuth tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never logged.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.
  • The app does not use cookies and does not collect data from visitors of this website.

Retention and deletion

Stored records are kept while the channel is operated and are deleted on request or when the project ends. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page; after revocation the app can no longer access YouTube data, and stored API data is deleted within 30 days.
